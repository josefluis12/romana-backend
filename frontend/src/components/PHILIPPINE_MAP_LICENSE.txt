The Philippine administrative boundary data in philippine-map-data.ts is a
simplified derivative of the regions GeoJSON published by the Barangay
Boundaries Repository. That dataset is aligned to the 2023-10-24 PSGC snapshot
and derives its boundaries from PSA/NAMRIA data and Philippines JSON Maps.
